01 — About

I design interfaces people don't have to think twice about — then help build them.

I’m a UI/UX Designer passionate about creating intuitive, accessible, and visually engaging digital experiences. My approach combines user research, information architecture, user flows, wireframing, interaction design, prototyping, and usability evaluation to transform user needs into simple and meaningful experiences.

I use Figma to create wireframes, high-fidelity interfaces, design systems, and interactive prototypes. With a background in Computer Science and knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js, Vite, Bootstrap, and Tailwind CSS, I bring both design thinking and technical understanding to the products I create. I’m passionate about solving real-world problems through thoughtful design and continuously improving experiences based on user needs and feedback.
